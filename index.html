How It Fits Together

1

Choose Your Tier, Not Your Program

There is one program. Every session is written five ways — dumbbells and a bench, dumbbells alone, kettlebells, bands, or nothing at all. Look at what is in the room and pick the tier, not a different week.

2

Core Lives Inside the Sessions

21 loaded core sets a week sit inside the training days, which builds more than the old thirty unloaded ones did. The standalone abs routine is kept for reference, not scheduled.

3

Effort, Not Reps

Every exercise carries a rep window and a target reps-in-reserve. Reps are the output; proximity to failure is the instruction. That is what makes the program survive a hotel rack that tops out at 20 lbs.
