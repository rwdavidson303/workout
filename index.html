How They Work Together

1

Choose Your Goal

The kettlebell program builds all-around strength and conditioning; the dumbbell program is a dedicated cut to lose fat and reveal muscle. Pick the one that matches your goal and your equipment.

2

Complementary Core Work

Pair either full-body program with the abs routine on alternate days. Ab sessions are 15-20 minutes — short enough to serve as active recovery.

3

Built-In Periodization

Both programs use 3 distinct day types that hit strength, hypertrophy, and conditioning. Sunday rest. Science-backed for maximum results.
