Dumbbell Exercise Library

Every movement in the cut program — mainstream lifts only, with form cues & tutorials

Primary Lifts (Illustrated Form Guides)

1

Bulgarian Split Squat

Compound Lower

Rear foot elevated on bench — lower until rear knee nearly touches floor

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand 2 feet in front of bench, place rear foot laces-down on bench
  • Hold dumbbells at sides, shoulders back and down
  • Lower straight down — front shin stays vertical
  • Rear knee drops toward floor, not forward
  • Drive through front heel to stand, squeeze glute at top
  • Keep torso upright — slight forward lean is OK
  • If balance is an issue, use lighter weights until stable
Used in: Optional alternative — swap for Reverse Lunge or Goblet Squat for variety
2

DB Romanian Deadlift

Compound Lower

Hinge at hips, slide DBs down thighs — stop when you feel hamstring stretch

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand with feet hip-width, soft bend in knees (10-15 degrees)
  • Push hips BACK, not down — this is a hinge, not a squat
  • DBs slide along the front of your thighs, staying close to body
  • Lower until you feel a strong hamstring stretch (usually mid-shin)
  • Keep back flat — neutral spine throughout
  • Drive hips forward to stand, squeeze glutes at top
  • Think "close the car door with your hips"
Used in: Strength (M) 3×10 — Hypertrophy (W) SS6b 3×12
3

DB Hip Thrust

Compound Lower

Upper back on bench, DB on hip crease — drive through heels, squeeze glutes at top

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Sit on floor with upper back against bench edge, knees bent, feet flat
  • Place DB on hip crease, hold in place with both hands
  • Drive through heels to extend hips until thighs are parallel to floor
  • Squeeze glutes hard at the top for 1-2 seconds
  • Lower hips under control — don't just drop down
  • Keep chin tucked slightly — look forward, not at ceiling
Used in: Optional glute accessory — add to any day for extra lower-body work
4

Reverse Lunge

Compound Lower

Step backward, not forward — less knee shear, more glute activation

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand tall with DBs at sides, step one foot BACKWARD
  • Lower until rear knee nearly touches floor (both knees at ~90 degrees)
  • Front shin stays vertical — knee doesn't shoot past toes
  • Drive through front heel to return to start
  • Alternate legs or complete all reps on one side, then switch
  • Keep torso upright throughout — core braced
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) SS6a 3×12/leg
5

Incline DB Press (30°)

Compound Push

Bench at 30° incline — 91% MVC for upper chest activation

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Set bench to 30 degrees (one or two notches from flat)
  • Feet flat on floor, shoulder blades pinched back and down
  • Start with DBs at chest height, palms forward
  • Press up and slightly together — don't clang them at top
  • Lower to deep stretch at chest level (deeper than barbell allows)
  • 1-second pause at bottom on strength day
  • Maintain natural arch in lower back — don't flatten
Used in: Strength (M) 4×6-8 — Hypertrophy (W) SS1a 3×12
6

One-Arm DB Row

Compound Pull

Hand and knee on bench — pull DB to hip, squeeze shoulder blade

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Place one hand and same-side knee on bench for support
  • Free arm hangs straight down holding DB, back flat like a table
  • Pull DB to your hip (not your armpit) — leads with elbow
  • Squeeze shoulder blade toward spine for 1 second at top
  • Lower under control — don't just drop the weight
  • Avoid rotating torso — hips and shoulders stay square
  • Think "start a lawnmower" motion
Used in: Strength (M) 4×8/arm — Hypertrophy (W) SS2b 3×12/arm — Metabolic (F) Circuit A 12/arm
7

Chest-Supported Row

Compound Pull

Face-down on incline bench (30-45°) — row DBs to ribcage, squeeze shoulder blades

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Set bench to 30-45 degrees, lie face-down with chest supported
  • Let arms hang straight down, holding DBs with neutral or pronated grip
  • Row DBs up to ribcage, leading with elbows
  • Squeeze shoulder blades together at the top for 1 second
  • Eliminates momentum — strict back activation only
  • Lower under control, feel the stretch at bottom
Used in: Strength (M) 3×10 — Hypertrophy (W) SS1b 3×12
8

Standing Overhead Press

Compound Push

Standing engages core as stabilizer — 79% MVC for shoulders

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand with feet shoulder-width, core braced tight
  • DBs start at shoulder height, palms facing forward
  • Press straight up to full lockout — biceps near ears
  • Don't arch back — squeeze glutes to prevent leaning
  • Lower under control back to shoulders
  • Breathe out on the press, in on the lower
Used in: Strength (M) seated 3×8 — see also Arnold Press & Thruster
9

Incline Curl

Isolation

Incline angle stretches long head — higher activation than standing curl

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Set bench to 45-55 degrees, sit back fully
  • Let arms hang straight down — feel the bicep stretch
  • Curl DBs up by bending elbows only — no swinging
  • Squeeze biceps hard at the top for 1 second
  • Lower slowly (3 sec on strength day) — this is where growth happens
  • Keep upper arms stationary — don't let elbows drift forward
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) SS4a 3×12
11

Overhead Tricep Extension

Isolation

One DB, both hands — lower behind head, extend to lockout

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Hold one DB with both hands (diamond grip on the top plate)
  • Start with DB overhead, arms fully extended
  • Lower behind head by bending elbows only (3-sec negative)
  • Keep elbows pointing forward — don't let them flare wide
  • Feel full tricep stretch at bottom, then extend back to lockout
  • Upper arms stay stationary — only forearms move
Used in: Metabolic (F) Circuit B 15 reps
13

Lateral Raise

Accessory

Raise to shoulder height only — peak medial deltoid activation

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand with slight forward lean (5-10 degrees)
  • DBs at sides, slight bend in elbows (locked throughout)
  • Raise arms out to sides, leading with elbows not hands
  • Stop at shoulder height — going higher risks impingement
  • Pause 1 second at top, lower under control (2 sec)
  • Think "pour water from a pitcher" at the top
  • Don't use momentum — if you're swinging, go lighter
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) SS5b 3×15 — Metabolic (F) Circuit B 15 reps
14

Bent-Over Rear Delt Fly

Accessory

85% MVC posterior deltoid — critical for shoulder balance and posture

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Hinge forward at hips to 45 degrees, flat back
  • DBs hang straight down, slight bend in elbows
  • Raise arms out to sides in a reverse fly motion
  • Squeeze shoulder blades together at top for 1 second
  • Lower under control — don't just drop the weights
  • Keep chest pointing at floor throughout
  • Think "spread your wings"
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) SS3b 3×15
15

Goblet Squat

Compound Lower

Quads, Glutes & Core

Hold one DB vertically at your chest — squat to full depth, chest tall

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Hold one DB by the top end, against your chest, elbows down
  • Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out
  • Sit hips back and down, knees tracking over toes
  • Go to full depth — thighs at least parallel
  • Keep chest tall and core braced; the DB counterbalances you
  • Drive through mid-foot and heels to stand
Used in: Strength (M) leg primer 3×10 — Metabolic (F) Circuit A 15 reps

More Upper-Body Movements

16

Flat DB Bench Press

Compound Push

Mid Chest, Front Delts, Triceps

Flat bench — press both DBs from a deep chest stretch to lockout

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie flat, feet planted, shoulder blades pinched back and down
  • Start with DBs at chest level, palms facing forward
  • Press up and slightly together — don't clang them at the top
  • Lower to a deep stretch at chest level (deeper than a barbell allows)
  • Keep a natural arch in the lower back, wrists stacked over elbows
  • Control the lower — that's where the chest works hardest
Used in: Strength (M) 3×8-10
17

DB Chest Fly

Isolation

Chest (Stretch & Squeeze)

Wide arc with a soft elbow bend — open to a stretch, hug back together

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie on a flat or low-incline bench, DBs pressed above the chest, palms facing each other
  • Keep a fixed, soft bend in the elbows the entire time
  • Open the arms in a wide arc until you feel a deep chest stretch
  • Squeeze the chest to bring the DBs back together over the chest
  • Think "hugging a barrel" — the elbow angle never changes
  • Go lighter than you press; this is a stretch and squeeze, not a grind
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) SS2a 3×12
18

Arnold Press

Compound Push

All Three Shoulder Heads

Start palms-in at the chin, rotate to palms-forward as you press overhead

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Seated or standing, start with DBs at chin height, palms facing you
  • As you press up, rotate the palms to face forward
  • Finish at full lockout overhead, biceps near the ears
  • Reverse the rotation on the way down to the start
  • The rotation recruits front and side delts in one rep
  • Brace the core; don't lean back to cheat the weight up
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) SS3a 3×12 — Metabolic (F) Circuit B 12 reps
19

Push-Ups

Compound Push

Chest, Triceps, Core

Body in a straight line — lower the chest to the floor, press back up

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Hands slightly wider than shoulders, body in a straight line head to heels
  • Brace the core and squeeze the glutes — no sagging hips
  • Lower until the chest nearly touches the floor, elbows ~45° from the body
  • Press back to full lockout
  • Too easy? Elevate the feet. Too hard? Drop to the knees to keep moving
  • Full range every rep beats half-reps for show
Used in: Metabolic (F) Circuit A 15 reps
20

Skull Crusher (Lying Tricep Extension)

Isolation

Triceps (All Heads)

Lying down — lower DBs beside the head by bending only the elbows, extend to lockout

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie on a flat bench, press two DBs up over the chest, palms facing each other
  • Keep the upper arms vertical and still — only the forearms move
  • Bend the elbows to lower the DBs beside or just behind the head
  • Stop at a comfortable stretch, then extend back to lockout
  • Keep the elbows tucked — don't let them flare wide
  • Control the lower; this protects the elbows and builds the triceps
Used in: Strength (M) SS 3×10 — Hypertrophy (W) SS4b 3×12
21

Hammer Curl

Isolation

Biceps & Brachialis (Arm Thickness)

Neutral grip (palms facing each other) — curl without swinging

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand tall, DBs at sides, palms facing each other (thumbs up)
  • Keep that neutral grip the whole rep — no rotation
  • Curl by bending the elbows only; upper arms stay pinned to the sides
  • Squeeze at the top, lower under control
  • The neutral grip builds the brachialis, which pushes the biceps up and adds arm thickness
  • If you're swinging, go lighter
Used in: Strength (M) SS 3×10 — Hypertrophy (W) SS5a 3×12 — Metabolic (F) Circuit B 12 reps
22

DB Pullover

Compound Pull

Lats & Chest (Ribcage Expansion)

One DB held over the chest — lower it back over the head, pull it back

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie flat on a bench, hold one DB with both hands over the chest
  • Keep a slight, fixed bend in the elbows
  • Lower the DB in an arc back over and behind the head until you feel a lat/chest stretch
  • Pull it back over the chest using the lats and chest, not the arms
  • Keep the hips down and ribs from flaring — brace the core
  • A great upper-body stretch and a movement most people skip
Used in: Optional accessory — swap into Hypertrophy (W) for variety
23

Renegade Row

Compound Pull

Back + Core (Anti-Rotation)

Plank on two DBs — row one DB to the hip while bracing against rotation, alternate

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Start in a high plank gripping two DBs, hands under shoulders
  • Set the feet wider than normal for a stable base
  • Row one DB to the hip while keeping the hips square to the floor
  • Resist the urge to twist — the anti-rotation is the core work
  • Lower under control, then row the other side
  • Use moderate weight; stability comes before load here
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) finisher 10 total

Core & Abs

24

Weighted Crunch

Core

Upper Abs

DB held on the chest — curl the ribcage toward the hips, slow down

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat
  • Hold a DB against the chest or on the upper chest
  • Curl the shoulders up by shortening the abs — ribs toward hips
  • Don't pull on the neck; eyes to the ceiling, chin off the chest
  • Lower slowly — the negative builds the abs
  • Adding load is how the abs get thicker and show more
Used in: Strength (M) Core Block 3×15
25

Lying Leg Raise

Core

Lower Abs

On your back — raise straight legs to vertical, lower slowly without arching

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie flat, hands under the hips or at the sides for support
  • Keep the legs straight (slight knee bend is fine) and together
  • Raise the legs to vertical using the lower abs
  • Lower slowly — stop before the low back arches off the floor
  • Press the low back down the entire set
  • Too hard? Bend the knees more, or do hanging knee raises if a bar is available
Used in: Strength (M) Core Block 3×12
26

Reverse Crunch

Core

Lower Abs

Curl the hips off the floor, knees toward the chest — control the lower

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie on your back, knees bent to ~90°, hands at the sides or under the hips
  • Curl the hips and pelvis up off the floor, bringing the knees toward the chest
  • The movement is the pelvis tilting, not just the legs swinging
  • Lower slowly — don't let the feet crash down
  • Keep it controlled; momentum takes the abs out of it
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) Core Block 3×15
27

Bicycle Crunch

Core

Upper Abs & Obliques

Opposite elbow to opposite knee — slow, full rotation each rep

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie on your back, hands lightly behind the head, shoulders lifted off the floor
  • Bring one knee in while rotating the opposite elbow toward it
  • Extend the other leg out straight, just off the floor
  • Alternate sides in a slow, deliberate pedal — not a race
  • Rotate from the torso, don't yank the neck with the hands
  • One of the highest-activation ab exercises when done slowly
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) Core Block 3×20 — Metabolic (F) Core Finisher 20 reps
28

DB Russian Twist

Core

Obliques

Lean back holding a DB — rotate it from hip to hip under control

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Sit with knees bent, lean the torso back to ~45° (feet up if you can balance)
  • Hold one DB with both hands at the chest
  • Rotate the torso to tap the DB toward the floor beside one hip, then the other
  • Move from the waist — the rotation is the work, not arm-swinging
  • Keep the chest tall and back flat, not rounded
  • Count one rep per side or per pair — stay consistent week to week
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) Core Block 3×20
29

DB Side Bend

Core

Obliques

One DB at your side — bend sideways toward it, then pull straight up

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Stand tall, hold one DB in one hand at your side, other hand behind the head
  • Bend sideways toward the DB, lowering it down the leg
  • Pull straight back up by contracting the opposite obliques
  • Move only in the side-to-side plane — no leaning forward or back
  • Finish all reps on one side, then switch the DB to the other hand
  • Use moderate weight; chasing huge loads here can thicken the waist
Used in: Optional oblique accessory — add to any core block
30

Plank

Core

Whole Core (Anti-Extension)

Forearms down, body in a straight line — brace hard and hold

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Forearms on the floor, elbows under the shoulders
  • Body in a straight line from head to heels — no sagging or piking
  • Squeeze the abs, glutes, and quads the entire hold
  • Keep the neck neutral, eyes down at the floor
  • Breathe steadily; don't hold your breath
  • Quality of the brace beats total time — stop when form breaks
Used in: Strength (M) Core Block 3×45s — Metabolic (F) Core Finisher 45s
31

Side Plank

Core

Obliques (Anti-Lateral-Flexion)

On one forearm — hips high, straight line, hold each side

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie on your side, elbow under the shoulder, feet stacked or staggered
  • Lift the hips so the body is a straight line, side-on to the floor
  • Drive the bottom hip up — don't let it sag toward the floor
  • Keep the top shoulder stacked over the bottom one
  • Hold for time, then switch sides
  • Carves the obliques and the line along the waist
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) Core Block 3×30s/side
32

V-Up

Core

Full Abs (Upper + Lower)

Reach hands to feet, folding into a "V" — lower with control

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie flat, arms extended overhead, legs straight
  • Simultaneously lift the legs and torso, reaching the hands toward the feet
  • Body folds into a "V", balancing on the glutes
  • Lower both halves under control back to the floor
  • Hits the upper and lower abs together — scale to tucked knees if needed
  • Keep the movement smooth, not a jerky snap
Used in: Metabolic (F) Core Finisher 12 reps
33

Flutter Kicks

Core

Lower Abs

Legs straight just off the floor — small fast alternating kicks

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Lie flat, hands under the hips for low-back support
  • Lift both legs a few inches off the floor, straight
  • Kick in small, quick alternating up-and-down motions
  • Keep the low back pressed into the floor the entire set
  • If the back arches, raise the legs higher to ease the leverage
  • Constant tension on the lower abs — for time, not reps
Used in: Metabolic (F) Core Finisher 30s
34

Mountain Climbers

Core / Conditioning

Abs + Cardio

High plank — drive the knees to the chest fast, alternating, hips low

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Start in a high plank, hands under the shoulders, body straight
  • Drive one knee toward the chest, then switch quickly
  • Keep the hips low and level — don't let them pike up
  • Stay on the balls of the feet, core braced throughout
  • The faster the pace, the more it becomes cardio — control the form first
  • Hits the abs while keeping the heart rate high
Used in: Hypertrophy (W) finisher 30s — Metabolic (F) Core Finisher 30s

Conditioning & Finishers

35

DB Thruster

Conditioning

Full Body (Biggest Burn)

Front squat into an overhead press in one fluid motion

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Hold DBs at the shoulders, palms facing in, feet shoulder-width
  • Squat to full depth, chest tall
  • Drive up explosively and use that momentum to press the DBs overhead
  • Lock out overhead, then bring the DBs back to the shoulders
  • One continuous motion squat-to-press — that's what spikes the burn
  • Moderate weight; this is a conditioning movement, not a max lift
Used in: Metabolic (F) Power Primer 3×12
36

DB Swing

Conditioning

Glutes, Hamstrings, Conditioning

Hinge and snap the hips to swing one DB to shoulder height

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Hold one DB by one end with both hands, feet shoulder-width
  • Hinge at the hips (don't squat), letting the DB swing back between the legs
  • Snap the hips forward explosively to drive the DB up to shoulder height
  • The power comes from the glutes and hips, not the arms or shoulders
  • Let the DB float at the top; control it back into the next hinge
  • Keep the back flat and core braced the entire set
Used in: Metabolic (F) Circuit A 15 reps
37

Farmer's Walk

Carry

Grip, Traps, Core, Conditioning

Hold heavy DBs at your sides — walk tall with purpose

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • Pick up the heaviest DBs you can carry, one in each hand
  • Stand tall: shoulders back and down, chest up, core braced
  • Walk with controlled steps for the prescribed time
  • Don't lean or shrug excessively — let the traps and grip hold the load
  • Breathe steadily; keep a strong, upright posture throughout
  • Builds a vice grip and a braced midsection while adding conditioning
Used in: Strength (M) Finisher 3×40s
38

Burpee

Conditioning

Full Body (Optional Burnout)

Squat, kick to a plank, push-up, jump the feet in, jump up

Watch Tutorial

Form Cues

  • From standing, squat and place the hands on the floor
  • Kick or step the feet back into a plank
  • Optional push-up, then jump or step the feet back to the hands
  • Stand and jump at the top (or just stand tall to lower impact)
  • Keep the core braced on the plank phase — no sagging hips
  • Step the feet instead of jumping to scale it down
Used in: Metabolic (F) optional finisher 2×10
