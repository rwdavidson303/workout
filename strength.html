Monday — Strength & Preservation

Strength Workout

Goal: Keep your muscle — lift heavy enough that your body holds size while fat comes off
Rest: 90-120 sec between sets
Tempo: 3-1-1 (3s down, 1s pause, 1s up)
Time: ~55-60 minutes

This is the day that protects everything you've built. Pick loads where the last 1-2 reps are genuinely hard — that effort is the signal that tells your body to keep its muscle in a deficit. Upper body leads; one leg primer keeps the engine running.

Block 1 — Strength Lifts (Straight Sets)
# Exercise Sets x Reps Weight Rest Notes Demo
1 Goblet Squat (leg primer) 3 x 10 Moderate 90s One DB at chest, full depth. Wakes up the legs and core to start. Form Cues
2 Incline DB Press (30°) 4 x 6-8 Heavy 120s Your heaviest press. Lower to chest, 1-sec pause, drive to lockout. Form Cues
3 One-Arm DB Row 4 x 8/arm Heavy 90s Hand and knee on bench. Pull to hip, squeeze shoulder blade 1 sec. Form Cues
4 Flat DB Bench Press 3 x 8-10 Heavy 90s Mid-chest mass. Lower with control, deep stretch, press together at top. Form Cues
5 Chest-Supported Row 3 x 10 Moderate-Heavy 90s Face-down on incline bench. Strict back work, no momentum. Form Cues
6 Seated Overhead Press 3 x 8 Moderate-Heavy 90s Press straight up to lockout. Brace core, no excess back arch. Form Cues
7 DB Romanian Deadlift 3 x 10 Moderate 90s Hinge at hips, soft knees. DBs slide down thighs. Feel hamstring stretch. Form Cues
8a SSHammer Curl 3 x 10 Moderate — Neutral grip. No swing — biceps and forearms do the work. Form Cues
8b SSSkull Crusher 3 x 10 Moderate 75s Lying tricep extension. Lower DBs beside head, extend to lockout. Form Cues
Block 2 — Core (2-3 Rounds, 30s Rest Between Rounds)
# Exercise Reps / Time Load Notes Demo
9a Weighted Crunch 15 DB on chest Slow and controlled. Crunch the ribs toward the hips, don't yank the neck. Form Cues
9b Lying Leg Raise 12 Bodyweight Lower legs slowly, keep low back pressed to the floor. Lower abs. Form Cues
9c Plank 45 sec Bodyweight Straight line head to heels. Squeeze abs and glutes the whole time. Form Cues
Block 3 — Finisher
# Exercise Sets x Time Weight Rest Notes
10 Farmer's Walk 3 x 40 sec Heaviest DBs 45s Walk tall, shoulders back, core braced. Grip, traps, and conditioning in one.
~28 working sets + core circuit — heavy loads, the day that keeps your muscle
