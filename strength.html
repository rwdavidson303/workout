Monday — Session A

Push-Lead Upper

Goal: Add upper-body muscle. This is the heavy day, and heavy is what actually builds the shape.
Rest: 90-120 sec between sets
Tempo: 3-1-1 (3s down, 1s pause, 1s up)
Time: ~50-55 minutes

The heaviest session of the week. Pick loads where the last 1-2 reps are genuinely hard and add weight or reps whenever you can. On a recomp at maintenance calories, this day is where the new muscle comes from.

Dumbbells + Bench. The default. Every movement at full range.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Incline DB Press (30°) 4 x 6-8 Heavy 120s Your heaviest press. Lower to the chest, 1-sec pause, drive to lockout. Form Cues
2 Chest-Supported Row 4 x 8-10 Heavy 90s Face-down on the incline bench. Strict, no momentum, squeeze 1 sec. Form Cues
3 Flat DB Bench Press 3 x 8-10 Heavy 90s Deep stretch at the bottom, press the dumbbells together at the top. Form Cues
4 One-Arm DB Row 3 x 8/arm Heavy 90s Hand and knee on bench. Pull to the hip, full stretch at the bottom. Form Cues
5 Seated Overhead Press 3 x 8 Moderate-Heavy 90s Press straight up to lockout. Brace the core, no excess back arch. Form Cues
6 Skull Crusher 3 x 10 Moderate 75s Lower beside the head, elbows tucked, extend to lockout. Form Cues
7 Incline Curl 3 x 10 Moderate 75s Bench at 45°, arms hanging back. Full biceps stretch, no swing. Form Cues
8 Weighted Crunch 3 x 12 DB on chest 45s Loaded and slow. If 12 is easy, add weight, not reps. Form Cues
9 Farmer's Walk 3 x 40 sec Heaviest DBs 45s Walk tall, shoulders back. Grip, traps, and conditioning in one. Form Cues

Dumbbells, No Bench. Floor presses replace incline and flat. Same stimulus, shorter range at the bottom.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 DB Floor Press 4 x 8 Heavy 120s Lie on the floor, upper arms touch down, pause, press. Safest heavy press with no bench. Form Cues
2 Bent-Over Two-DB Row 4 x 10 Heavy 90s Hinge to 45°, flat back. Row both DBs to the ribs, squeeze 1 sec. Form Cues
3 DB Floor Fly 3 x 12 Light-Moderate 75s Wide arc, slight elbow bend. Upper arms kiss the floor, squeeze at the top. Form Cues
4 One-Arm DB Row 3 x 8/arm Heavy 90s Free hand on your knee or a chair. Pull to the hip, no twisting. Form Cues
5 Standing Overhead Press 3 x 8 Moderate-Heavy 90s Squeeze glutes and brace hard. Standing makes the core work too. Form Cues
6 OH Tricep Extension 3 x 10 Moderate 75s One DB, both hands, full stretch behind the head. Form Cues
7 Standing DB Curl 3 x 10 Moderate 75s Elbows pinned to your sides. Control the lowering. Form Cues
8 Weighted Crunch 3 x 12 DB on chest 45s Loaded and slow. Add weight before you add reps. Form Cues
9 Farmer's Walk 3 x 40 sec Heaviest DBs 45s Walk tall, shoulders back, core braced. Form Cues

Bands Only. Anchor the band in a door hinge. Log the band's rated pounds as your weight.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Band Chest Press 4 x 12 Heavy band 90s Band behind your back, press forward and slightly in. Squeeze at lockout. Form Cues
2 Band Seated Row 4 x 12 Heavy band 90s Anchor at chest height. Row to the ribs, pull the shoulder blades together. Form Cues
3 Band Incline Press 3 x 12 Moderate band 75s Anchor low, press up and forward at a 30° angle. Upper chest. Form Cues
4 Band One-Arm Row 3 x 12/arm Moderate band 75s One side at a time, full stretch, pull to the hip. Form Cues
5 Band Overhead Press 3 x 12 Moderate band 75s Stand on the band, press overhead to lockout. Form Cues
6 Band Tricep Pushdown 3 x 15 Light band 60s Anchor high. Elbows pinned, extend fully, squeeze at the bottom. Form Cues
7 Band Curl 3 x 12 Moderate band 60s Stand on the band. Elbows still, full range. Form Cues
8 Band Crunch 3 x 15 Light band 45s Anchor high behind you, crunch the ribs to the hips against the band. Form Cues
9 Hollow Body Hold 3 x 30 sec Bodyweight 45s Low back pinned to the floor. Arms and legs long. Shake is normal. Form Cues

No Equipment. Hotel room. Bed, desk, door, towel. Log reps, chase more of them each time.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Decline Push-Up 4 x 12 Feet on the bed 90s Feet elevated shifts load to the upper chest and shoulders. Slow down, chest to floor. Form Cues
2 Door-Handle Row 4 x 12 Bodyweight 90s Both handles of a solid door, towel over the top edge. Feet forward, lean back, row your chest to the door. Form Cues
3 Push-Up 3 x 15 Bodyweight 75s Full range, chest to the floor, body in one line. 3 seconds down. Form Cues
4 Towel Row (one arm) 3 x 12/arm Bodyweight 75s Towel round a door handle, one hand, lean back and pull. Lean further to make it harder. Form Cues
5 Pike Push-Up 3 x 10 Feet on the bed 75s Hips high, head between the hands. The best shoulder press you can do with nothing. Form Cues
6 Bed-Edge Dip 3 x 15 Bodyweight 60s Hands on the bed edge behind you, feet out. Elbows straight back, not flared. Form Cues
7 Towel Curl 3 x 12 Self-resisted 60s Towel under one foot, curl against your own downward pull. 4 seconds up, 4 down. Form Cues
8 Hollow Body Hold 3 x 30 sec Bodyweight 45s Low back pinned, arms and legs long. Form Cues
9 Suitcase Carry 3 x 40 sec Loaded bag 45s Pack the carry-on heavy. One hand, walk tall, don't lean. Form Cues
