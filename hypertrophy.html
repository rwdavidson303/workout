Wednesday — Session B

Pull-Lead Upper

Goal: Build the back. Lat width is what makes the waist look smaller and the shoulders look wider.
Rest: 60-90 sec between sets
Tempo: 2-1-1 (2s down, 1s pause, 1s up)
Time: ~50 minutes

Back leads because back was the hole in the old program. Every set is a stretch and a squeeze: full extension at the bottom, one-second hold at the contraction. This is the session that builds the V.

Dumbbells + Bench. The default. Every movement at full range.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 DB Pullover 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Across the bench, hips low. Reach back for a deep lat stretch, pull over the chest. Form Cues
2 Chest-Supported Row 4 x 12 Moderate 75s Face-down on the incline. Elbows wide for upper back, tucked for lats. Form Cues
3 One-Arm DB Row 3 x 12/arm Moderate 75s Let the shoulder blade travel. Stretch at the bottom, pull to the hip. Form Cues
4 Incline DB Press 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Full range, 1-sec pause at the chest stretch. Form Cues
5 Bent-Over Rear Delt Fly 3 x 15 Light 60s Hinged 45°. Lead with the elbows, squeeze the shoulder blades. Form Cues
6 Lateral Raise 3 x 15 Light 60s Elbows lead to shoulder height only. This is the width the shirt shows. Form Cues
7 Hammer Curl 3 x 12 Moderate 60s Neutral grip. Builds the thickness of the arm, not just the peak. Form Cues
8 OH Tricep Extension 3 x 12 Moderate 60s One DB, both hands, deep stretch behind the head. Form Cues
9 Lying Leg Raise 3 x 12 Bodyweight 45s Lower slowly, low back pressed down. Stop before the back arches. Form Cues

Dumbbells, No Bench. Floor presses replace incline and flat. Same stimulus, shorter range at the bottom.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 DB Pullover (floor) 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Flat on the floor. Shorter range than a bench but the lat stretch is still there. Form Cues
2 Bent-Over Two-DB Row 4 x 12 Moderate 75s Hinge to 45°, flat back, row to the ribs. Form Cues
3 One-Arm DB Row 3 x 12/arm Moderate 75s Free hand braced on a knee or chair. Form Cues
4 DB Floor Press 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Upper arms touch down, brief pause, press. Form Cues
5 Bent-Over Rear Delt Fly 3 x 15 Light 60s Hinged 45°, elbows lead. Form Cues
6 Lateral Raise 3 x 15 Light 60s Shoulder height only, no swinging. Form Cues
7 Hammer Curl 3 x 12 Moderate 60s Neutral grip, elbows pinned. Form Cues
8 OH Tricep Extension 3 x 12 Moderate 60s Full stretch behind the head. Form Cues
9 Lying Leg Raise 3 x 12 Bodyweight 45s Slow lower, low back down. Form Cues

Bands Only. Anchor the band in a door hinge. Log the band's rated pounds as your weight.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Band Lat Pulldown 4 x 12 Heavy band 75s Anchor over the door top. Kneel, pull to the collarbone, elbows down not back. Closest thing to a pull-up in a hotel. Form Cues
2 Band Seated Row 4 x 12 Heavy band 75s Anchor at chest height. Row to the ribs, hold the squeeze. Form Cues
3 Band One-Arm Row 3 x 12/arm Moderate band 75s Full stretch, let the shoulder blade travel forward and back. Form Cues
4 Band Chest Press 3 x 12 Moderate band 75s Band behind the back, press forward and slightly in. Form Cues
5 Band Face Pull 3 x 15 Light band 60s Anchor at eye height. Pull to the forehead, elbows high, rotate out. Rear delts. Form Cues
6 Band Lateral Raise 3 x 15 Light band 60s Stand on the band, raise to shoulder height. Form Cues
7 Band Hammer Curl 3 x 12 Moderate band 60s Neutral grip, elbows still. Form Cues
8 Band OH Tricep Extension 3 x 12 Moderate band 60s Anchor low behind you, extend overhead. Form Cues
9 Lying Leg Raise 3 x 12 Bodyweight 45s Slow lower, low back pinned. Form Cues

No Equipment. Hotel room. Bed, desk, door, towel. Log reps, chase more of them each time.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Door-Handle Row 4 x 15 Bodyweight 75s The main back movement. Walk the feet further forward to make it harder. Form Cues
2 Towel Row (one arm) 3 x 12/arm Bodyweight 75s One hand on a towel round the handle. Lean back further for more load. Form Cues
3 Sliding Lat Pullover 3 x 12 Towel on floor 75s Kneel, hands on a towel, slide it out overhead and pull it back. Lats under a long stretch. Form Cues
4 Wide Push-Up 3 x 15 Bodyweight 60s Hands wider than shoulders, chest to floor, 3 seconds down. Form Cues
5 Prone T Raise 3 x 15 Bodyweight 45s Face down, arms straight out to the sides. Lift, squeeze 1 sec, lower. Rear delts and mid-back. Form Cues
6 Prone Y Raise 3 x 15 Bodyweight 45s Same position, arms at 45° overhead. Lower traps and rear delts. Form Cues
7 Towel Curl 3 x 12/arm Self-resisted 60s 4 seconds up against your own resistance, 4 seconds down. Form Cues
8 Diamond Push-Up 3 x 12 Bodyweight 60s Hands in a diamond under the chest. Triceps. Form Cues
9 Lying Leg Raise 3 x 15 Bodyweight 45s Slow, controlled, low back down. Form Cues
28 sets — back 10, chest 3, delts 6, arms 6, core 3
