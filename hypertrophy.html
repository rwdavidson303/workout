Wednesday — Hypertrophy & Definition

Hypertrophy Workout

Goal: Build detail in chest, back, shoulders & arms while keeping the heart rate up
Rest: 60-75 sec between supersets
Tempo: 2-1-1 (2s down, 1s pause, 1s up)
Time: ~50-55 minutes

Agonist-antagonist supersets — perform a then b back-to-back with no rest between, then rest before the next superset. Chase a deep pump and take each set to within 1-2 reps of failure. The short rest keeps calories burning while you build.

# Exercise Sets x Reps Weight Rest Notes Demo
1a SS1Incline DB Press 3 x 12 Moderate — Full ROM, 1-sec pause at the chest stretch. Press to lockout. Form Cues
1b SS1Chest-Supported Row 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Row both DBs to the ribcage, squeeze 1 sec. Strict, no momentum. Form Cues
2a SS2DB Chest Fly 3 x 12 Light-Moderate — Wide arc, slight elbow bend. Deep stretch, squeeze at the top. Form Cues
2b SS2One-Arm DB Row 3 x 12/arm Moderate 60s Pull to hip, full stretch at the bottom. Lats do the work. Form Cues
3a SS3Arnold Press 3 x 12 Light-Moderate — Rotate from palms-in to palms-forward as you press. Hits all three delt heads. Form Cues
3b SS3Bent-Over Rear Delt Fly 3 x 15 Light 60s Hinged 45°. Squeeze shoulder blades. Rear delts balance the look. Form Cues
4a SS4Incline Curl 3 x 12 Light-Moderate — Bench at 45°, arms hang back. Full stretch on the biceps, no swing. Form Cues
4b SS4Skull Crusher 3 x 12 Light-Moderate 60s Lower DBs beside the head, extend to lockout. Keep elbows tucked. Form Cues
5a SS5Hammer Curl 3 x 12 Moderate — Neutral grip for thicker-looking arms. Control the lower. Form Cues
5b SS5Lateral Raise 3 x 15 Light 60s Lead with elbows to shoulder height only. Caps the shoulders — the look that widens the frame. Form Cues
6a SS6Reverse Lunge 3 x 12/leg Moderate — Step back, drop rear knee, drive through front heel. Leg maintenance dose. Form Cues
6b SS6DB Romanian Deadlift 3 x 12 Moderate 60s Hinge pattern, pause 1 sec at full stretch before driving up. Form Cues
