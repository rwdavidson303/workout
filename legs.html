Saturday — Session D

Legs + Carries

Goal: Maintain the legs in half an hour. Deliberately a maintenance dose, not a focus.
Rest: 60-90 sec between sets
Tempo: 2-0-1 (controlled down, drive up)
Time: ~30 minutes

Nine hard leg sets a week is enough to hold what you have while the upper body gets the attention. If a week goes sideways, this is the session to drop. Never drop Monday.

Dumbbells + Bench. The default. Every movement at full range.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Bulgarian Split Squat 3 x 10/leg Moderate 90s Rear foot on the bench. Drop straight down, drive through the front heel. Form Cues
2 DB Romanian Deadlift 3 x 10 Moderate-Heavy 90s Hinge at the hips, soft knees, DBs slide down the thighs. Feel the hamstrings. Form Cues
3 Reverse Lunge 3 x 12/leg Moderate 75s Step back, drop the rear knee, drive through the front heel. Form Cues
4 DB Calf Raise 3 x 15 Moderate 45s Full stretch at the bottom, 1-sec squeeze at the top. Form Cues
5 Suitcase Carry 3 x 40 sec Heavy, one side 45s One DB, walk tall, resist the lean. Obliques and grip. Form Cues

Dumbbells, No Bench. Floor presses replace incline and flat. Same stimulus, shorter range at the bottom.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Bulgarian Split Squat 3 x 10/leg Moderate 90s Rear foot on a chair or the bed edge. Same movement, no bench needed. Form Cues
2 DB Romanian Deadlift 3 x 10 Moderate-Heavy 90s Hinge, soft knees, deep hamstring stretch. Form Cues
3 Reverse Lunge 3 x 12/leg Moderate 75s Step back, controlled, drive through the front heel. Form Cues
4 DB Calf Raise 3 x 15 Moderate 45s Full stretch, 1-sec squeeze. Form Cues
5 Suitcase Carry 3 x 40 sec Heavy, one side 45s Walk tall, resist the lean. Form Cues

Bands Only. Anchor the band in a door hinge. Log the band's rated pounds as your weight.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Band Split Squat 3 x 12/leg Moderate band 75s Stand on the band, handles at the shoulders. Rear foot elevated. Form Cues
2 Band Romanian Deadlift 3 x 12 Heavy band 75s Stand on the band, hinge, drive the hips forward. Form Cues
3 Band Reverse Lunge 3 x 12/leg Moderate band 60s Stand on the band with the front foot, step back. Form Cues
4 Band Glute Bridge 3 x 15 Heavy band 45s Band across the hips, drive up, squeeze 1 sec. Form Cues
5 Band Calf Raise 3 x 20 Moderate band 45s Stand on the band, rise onto the toes, hold. Form Cues

No Equipment. Hotel room. Bed, desk, door, towel. Log reps, chase more of them each time.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Bulgarian Split Squat 3 x 15/leg Rear foot on bed 75s No load needed at 15 reps a side. Slow down, 3 seconds descending. Form Cues
2 Single-Leg Romanian Deadlift 3 x 12/leg Bodyweight 60s Hinge on one leg, back leg straight behind. Balance is part of it. Form Cues
3 Reverse Lunge 3 x 15/leg Bodyweight 60s Controlled, no bouncing off the bottom. Form Cues
4 Single-Leg Glute Bridge 3 x 15/leg Bodyweight 45s One foot down, drive the hip up, squeeze 1 sec. Form Cues
5 Wall Sit 3 x 60 sec Bodyweight 45s Thighs parallel, back flat on the wall. Hold and breathe. Form Cues
6 Calf Raise 3 x 25 Bodyweight 30s Off a step or the floor. Full range, slow. Form Cues
9 leg sets + carries — maintenance dose, ~30 minutes
