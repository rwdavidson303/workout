Friday — Session C

Detail Upper

Goal: Chest, delts and arms at higher reps. The finishing coat on what Monday and Wednesday built.
Rest: 60-75 sec between sets
Tempo: 2-1-1 (2s down, 1s pause, 1s up)
Time: ~45-50 minutes

Lighter than Monday, higher reps, and taken close to failure. This is the pump-and-detail session. Stop 1-2 reps short of failure, not 5. The last few hard reps are the ones that count at this rep range.

Dumbbells + Bench. The default. Every movement at full range.

#ExerciseSets x RepsLoadRestNotesDemo
1 Incline DB Press 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Full range, pause at the stretch, press to lockout. Form Cues
2 DB Chest Fly 3 x 12 Light-Moderate 60s Wide arc, slight elbow bend. Deep stretch, squeeze at the top. Form Cues
3 Chest-Supported Row 3 x 12 Moderate 75s Strict, no momentum, 1-sec squeeze. Form Cues
4 DB Pullover 3 x 12 Moderate 60s Deep lat stretch overhead, pull over the chest. Form Cues
5 Arnold Press 3 x 12 Light-Moderate 75s Rotate palms-in to palms-forward as you press. All three delt heads. Form Cues
6 Lateral Raise 3 x 15 Light 60s Shoulder height only. Burn them out. Form Cues
7 Skull Crusher 3 x 12 Light-Moderate 60s Elbows tucked, lower beside the head. Form Cues
8 Hammer Curl 3 x 12 Moderate 60s Neutral grip, no swing even when tired. Form Cues
9 Side Plank 3 x 30 sec/side Bodyweight 45s Hips high, straight line. Obliques and waistline. Form Cues
