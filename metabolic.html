Friday — Metabolic Fat-Loss

Metabolic Workout

Goal: Burn calories during and for hours after — keep the heart rate high, keep moving
Rest: 30-45 sec between circuits
Tempo: 2-0-1 (controlled, fluid)
Time: ~45-55 minutes

Lighter loads, higher reps, short rest. A power primer first, then two upper-body-heavy circuits, then a non-stop core finisher. Pick weights you can move with good form while breathing hard — the goal is a high, steady heart rate, not heavy singles.

Block 1 — Power Primer (Straight Sets, 45s Rest)
# Exercise Sets x Reps Weight Rest Notes Demo
1 DB Thruster 3 x 12 Moderate 45s Squat to overhead press in one fluid motion. Full-body, biggest burn in the program. Form Cues
Block 2 — Circuit A (3-4 Rounds, 30s Rest Between Rounds)
# Exercise Reps Weight Notes Demo
2a Push-Ups 15 Bodyweight Full ROM, chest to floor. Drop to knees if needed to keep the rhythm. Form Cues
2b One-Arm DB Row 12/arm Light-Moderate Hinge forward, no bench needed. Keep the pace. Form Cues
2c DB Swing 15 Moderate Hip hinge and snap, DB to shoulder height. Power from the glutes, not the arms. Form Cues
2d Goblet Squat 15 Moderate One DB at chest, full depth, keep moving. Form Cues
Block 3 — Circuit B (3 Rounds, 30s Rest Between Rounds)
# Exercise Reps Weight Notes Demo
3a Arnold Press 12 Light Rotate as you press. Keep it smooth and continuous. Form Cues
3b Lateral Raise 15 Light Shoulder height only. Burn out the delts. Form Cues
3c Hammer Curl 12 Light-Moderate Neutral grip, no swing even when tired. Form Cues
3d Overhead Tricep Extension 15 Light One DB, both hands. Full stretch behind the head. Form Cues
Block 4 — Core Finisher Circuit (2-3 Rounds, Minimal Rest)
# Exercise Reps / Time Load Notes Demo
4a Mountain Climbers 30 sec Bodyweight Fast, hips low. Cardio and abs together. Form Cues
4b Bicycle Crunch 20 Bodyweight Opposite elbow to knee, controlled. Form Cues
4c V-Up 12 Bodyweight Reach hands to feet, full fold. Whole-ab hit. Form Cues
4d Flutter Kicks 30 sec Bodyweight Small fast kicks, low back pinned down. Lower abs. Form Cues
4e Plank 45 sec Bodyweight Hold tight to finish. Brace everything. Form Cues

Optional: Burpee Burnout

  • Feeling strong? Close with 2 rounds of 10 burpees, 30s rest between.
  • Pure conditioning — the fastest way to add calorie burn when you have gas left in the tank.
  • Skip it on low-energy days. The deficit makes some sessions feel harder than others; that's normal.
Primer + 2 circuits + core finisher — high heart rate, maximum calorie burn
